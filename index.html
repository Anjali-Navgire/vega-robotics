OUR STORY

Built to keep the sun's
promise honest

VEGA ROBOTICS began with a broader question: what happens to solar panels at the end of their life? While researching solar panels and their recycling process, we discovered that one of the biggest challenges actually begins much earlier — keeping panels clean. Dust and dirt continuously reduce panel performance, while regular cleaning can be time-consuming, labor-intensive, and difficult to manage at scale.

We also found that solar cleaning robots already exist, but many are too expensive for farmers and smaller solar installations. That led us to a simple goal: build a more affordable and practical robotic cleaning solution that makes automated solar panel cleaning accessible to more people. We started with a simple mechanical system, focusing on reliability and real-world testing first — and we'll keep improving it as we learn from every test.
